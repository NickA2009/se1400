The Isaac community and the Southern Utah Smash community are two groups I spend a lot of time in and would definitely recommend to anyone who enjoys gaming, competition, or just hanging out with other players. The Isaac community is a really cool and zany place where people talk about The Binding of Isaac, share item combinations, discuss strategies, and help each other learn more about the game. It’s full of creativity, weird interactions, and fun discussions that make the game even more interesting than it already is. Whether someone is new or experienced, there’s always something going on and plenty of people willing to share advice or just talk about runs. The Southern Utah Smash community is also a cool and zany environment, but more focused on competitive play and local meetups. Players coordinate matches, set up friendly competitions, and talk about improving their skills in Super Smash Bros. It’s a great place for anyone who wants to get better, find practice partners, or just enjoy playing with other people in the area. The atmosphere is welcoming, competitive, and always active with people looking to play or discuss the game. Alongside these communities, you can also find me through my socials.
